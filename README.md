TIDEBOUND is an original card duel. You are Wren, keeper of the last lantern-light; the Ink Baron, a collector who steals light, sits across the table. Win 2 duels to take the match — a duel ends when one side's 30 lanterns burn out.

Each turn you draw 2 cards and gain 1 ink (max 10). Strike for damage, Guard for block, or weave a Trick to draw, steal ink, or reflect the next hit. Four suits — Ember, Tide, Gale, Stone — are told apart by colour and shape, never by text alone, and they cycle: Ember beats Gale, Gale beats Tide, Tide beats Ember, Stone is neutral. The depth is in the combos: play two cards of the same suit for Harmony (+2), three different suits for Prism (draw 1, refund 1 ink), and play the suit that beats the Baron's telegraphed suit for Counter (+3). The Baron shows you his next suit before he plays it, so every hand is a read — and a chance to punish him for it.

Cards slide, flip and snap into place; damage numbers float; a win lands with a lantern flourish. Synthesised WebAudio bleeps only — no audio files, no network, no libraries, just canvas and ES modules.

## Controls

- Click a card or press `1–5` to play it (if you can pay its ink)
- `Space` end turn · `P` pause · `M` mute · `R` restart

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
