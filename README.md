BRASS BAZAAR is an original card duel set in a night market. You are Pip, a young peddler with a handcart and a loud voice; across the counter sits Madame Zinnia, who owns half the stalls already and wants the rest. Win 2 duels to take the match — a duel ends when one side's 30 patrons have all wandered off.

Each turn you draw 2 cards and your coins rise by 1 (max 10). Sell cards to pull patrons to your stall, Charm cards to guard your own crowd, or Haggle cards to draw, steal coins, or reflect the next hit back at her. Four stalls — Spice, Silk, Brass, Leaf — are told apart by colour and shape, never by text alone, and they undercut each other in a cycle: Spice beats Silk, Silk beats Brass, Brass beats Leaf, Leaf beats Spice.

The depth is in the reads and the combos: Zinnia always shows you which stall she reaches for next, so you can Undercut her (+3) by playing its counter. Two cards of one stall in a turn make a Stall Set (+2); three different stalls make a Grand Bazaar (draw 1, refund 1 coin); and every card you play fills the square until the Crowd hits 8 and Rush Hour strikes (next Sell +4). She grows grander every duel — Peddler, then Merchant, then Baroness — and her sells hit harder each time.

Cards snap onto the counter, damage numbers float, the crowd roars when Rush Hour lands, and a win ends in a shower of coins. Synthesised WebAudio sounds only — no audio files, no network, no libraries, just canvas and ES modules.

## Controls

- Click a card or press `1–7` to play it (if you can pay its coins)
- `Space` end turn · `P` pause · `M` mute · `R` restart

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
